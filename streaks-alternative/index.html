The best alternatives to Streaks

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Streaks is a polished one-time purchase. If you’d rather pay nothing, count bad habits in days without, or keep a streak that forgives an off day, here are the best alternatives, starting with Coutume.

1. Coutume: the best free alternative to Streaks

Coutume keeps what people like about Streaks, one tap to log, Apple Health and streaks, and adds habits to quit counted in days without, levels and milestones. Free, with no account, no ads and no subscription.

Why choose Coutume

  • Free, for good. No purchase, no subscription, no ads, and no habit limit behind a paywall.
  • Bad habits get their own logic. A habit to quit is kept by default: you only log the day you slip. Coutume counts your days without, the money saved and the urges you resisted.
  • A streak that forgives. One missed day a week doesn’t break your streak, so one off day doesn’t erase weeks of effort.
  • Something to aim for. Eleven levels, from Good intention to Legend, and eighteen milestones, earned with every day kept.
  • Private by default. No server, no analytics: your habits stay on your iPhone and in your private iCloud. We never receive them.

2. Way of Life

Best for a quick yes-or-no diary with strong charts. Each day you mark a habit green or red, and the charts show the trend over months. Around since 2010, on iPhone, iPad and Android. Free with a habit limit; Premium unlocks the rest.

3. HabitKit

Best for a private grid of your habits. No account, your data on your device, and a tile grid that fills in like a contribution graph. Free for 4 habits; Pro is monthly, yearly or lifetime.

4. Strides

Best for mixing habits with goals and targets. Besides habits, it tracks targets by a date, averages and projects, with over 150 templates, Apple Health, a Mac app and a web app. Free for 3 trackers; Strides Plus is a subscription or a lifetime purchase.

When Streaks may suit you better

Streaks has been around for years and runs on more devices. You may prefer it if you want to log from your Apple Watch, iPad or Mac, if you need habits due only on some days of the week, or if you already own it and it works for you.

Moving from Streaks to Coutume

Coutume doesn’t import from Streaks: add your habits again, it takes a few seconds each. Habits filled in by Apple Health catch up on their own, since Coutume reads the last seven days each time you open it.
