Support

A question, a number that looks off or an idea for Coutume? Write to us, we read every message.

Contact us

Write to us at hello@wibeset.ca. We usually reply within two business days, in English or French. For a problem, tell us your iPhone model and iOS version, and attach a screenshot if you can.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my habits stored?

On your iPhone, and in your private iCloud if iCloud is turned on for Coutume. Coutume works without an account or server: we can’t access your habits.

Why doesn’t Coutume count my steps?

Coutume needs your permission to read them. Go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Coutume, and turn on Steps, along with Exercise Minutes, Mindful Minutes and Water if you use them. Then open Coutume again.

My steps don’t match the Health app

Coutume uses the same daily total as Health, which counts your iPhone and Apple Watch only once. Apple Watch data can reach your iPhone a little later: each time you open Coutume, it reads the last seven days again and catches up.

I logged a slip or a glass by mistake

Touch and hold the habit’s ring on the day’s screen, then tap Undo last. To set a count exactly, choose Enter a value.

What’s the joker?

Your streak forgives one missed day per week, so a single off day doesn’t erase weeks of effort. Miss a second day in the same week and the streak starts over; your level and milestones stay.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget, then choose Coutume. It shows the day’s rings and your streak of perfect days, and also fits on the Lock Screen.

What’s the difference between archiving and deleting?

An archived habit leaves your day but keeps counting in your history, your level and your milestones; you can bring it back from Profile. Deleting a habit erases it and its history for good.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a habit from its page, or delete the app to erase its data from your iPhone. To erase the iCloud copy too, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage.

Is Coutume free?

Yes, with no ads, no subscription and no account.
