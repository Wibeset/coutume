Quit energy drinks, one day at a time

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Coutume is a free habit tracker for iPhone with a habit made for quitting: it counts your days without energy drinks, the money you save and the urges you resist. No account, no ads.

Kept by default, logged only when you slip

Most trackers want you to check a box every day. A habit to quit works the other way: each day counts as kept unless you log a slip. On a good day, there is nothing to do.

Days without, money saved, urges resisted

Coutume counts your days without energy drinks, the money you didn’t spend on cans, and the urges you resisted, so you can see them add up week after week.

A slip restarts the count, not everything

If you slip, the count of days without starts over. Your level, your milestones and your month stay: they count every day you kept.

Replace it with something

Quitting is easier with something in its place. Add a habit to build beside it, like 8 glasses of water or a walk, and fill its ring with a tap, or let Apple Health fill it for you.

Coutume is a habit tracker, not a medical app. If you are worried about your caffeine intake, talk to a doctor or a pharmacist.

Coutume is coming soon to the App Store, for iPhone: free, with no account, no ads and no subscription.
