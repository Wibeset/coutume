The best alternatives to HabitKit

Last updated: November 23, 2026

HabitKit is private and simple, and Coutume shares that spirit. But HabitKit’s free version stops at four habits, and Apple Health isn’t among its features. Here are the best alternatives, starting with Coutume.

1. Coutume: the best free alternative to HabitKit

Like HabitKit, Coutume works without an account and keeps your habits on your iPhone. It adds Apple Health, levels, milestones and a streak that forgives, and every habit is free.

Why choose Coutume

  • Free, for good. No purchase, no subscription, no ads, and no habit limit behind a paywall.
  • Apple Health does the logging. Steps, exercise minutes, mindful minutes and water fill in your habits on their own.
  • A streak that forgives. One missed day a week doesn’t break your streak, so one off day doesn’t erase weeks of effort.
  • Something to aim for. Eleven levels, from Good intention to Legend, and eighteen milestones, earned with every day kept.

2. Streaks

Best for a polished app across your Apple devices. A one-time purchase that runs on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Mac, with deep Apple Health support and up to 24 tasks, negative ones included. US$5.99, with no subscription.

3. Way of Life

Best for a quick yes-or-no diary with strong charts. Each day you mark a habit green or red, and the charts show the trend over months. Around since 2010, on iPhone, iPad and Android. Free with a habit limit; Premium unlocks the rest.

4. Habitify

Best for tracking on iPhone, Android and the web. Your habits live in an online account and sync everywhere, with detailed statistics. Free for 3 habits; the Plus and Pro plans unlock the rest, and Apple Health is in Pro.

When HabitKit may suit you better

HabitKit runs on Android too, and its tile grid shows months of habits at a glance. You may prefer it if you switch between an iPhone and an Android phone, or if that grid is what keeps you going.

Moving from HabitKit to Coutume

Coutume doesn’t import from HabitKit: add your habits again, it takes a few seconds each. Habits filled in by Apple Health catch up on their own, since Coutume reads the last seven days each time you open it.

Coutume is coming soon to the App Store, for iPhone: free, with no account, no ads and no subscription.
