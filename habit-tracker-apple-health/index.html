A habit tracker that Apple Health fills in for you

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Walk, and your steps habit fills itself. Coutume reads four kinds of data from Apple Health to log your habits on its own, so you only tap for the rest. Free, with no account and no ads.

Four habits that log themselves

Steps, exercise minutes, mindful minutes and water. Link a habit to one of them, like 10,000 steps or 8 glasses of water, and its ring fills as Health counts.

The same total as the Health app

Coutume uses the same daily total as Health, which counts your iPhone and Apple Watch only once. Apple Watch data can reach your iPhone a little later: each time you open Coutume, it reads the last seven days again and catches up.

Next to the habits you tap

Your Health habits sit with the others in your day: reading 20 pages, meditating, going to bed on time. One tap logs those; Health logs the rest.

Read only, and private

Coutume never writes to Health, and never reads anything you didn’t allow. The daily totals stay on your iPhone and in your private iCloud: they are never sent to us or used for advertising. You can change what Coutume reads at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Coutume.

Coutume is coming soon to the App Store, for iPhone: free, with no account, no ads and no subscription.
