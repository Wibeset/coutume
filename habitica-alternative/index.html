A Habitica alternative, with levels but no role-playing game

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Habitica turns your habits into a role-playing game. Coutume keeps the reward of levelling up, and drops the avatar, the gold and the account: a free habit tracker for iPhone, quiet and private.

Why choose Coutume

  • Progress without the game to manage. No gear to buy, no quest to follow: every day kept moves you toward the next level, from Good intention to Legend.
  • Gentle on a bad day. One missed day a week is forgiven, and a slip never takes away your level or your milestones.
  • Less to log. One tap fills a ring, and Apple Health fills in your steps, exercise, mindful minutes and water on its own.
  • No account, no one watching. Your habits stay on your iPhone and in your private iCloud.

When Habitica may suit you better

Habitica shines if the game is what keeps you going: if you enjoy an avatar to dress, quests to finish and friends counting on you in a party. It also works on Android and the web, and mixes habits with to-dos.

Coutume is coming soon to the App Store, for iPhone: free, with no account, no ads and no subscription.
