Privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Coutume works without an account or sign-up. Your habits, your history and what Coutume reads from Apple Health are stored on your iPhone and in your iCloud, and we never receive them.

This policy explains what information the Coutume app for iPhone handles, and why. Coutume is made by Wibeset, in Canada.

Apple Health

Your habits can say a lot about you, so we treat them as personal. If you allow it, Coutume reads four kinds of data from Apple Health to fill in your habits on its own: steps, exercise minutes, mindful minutes and water. It reads them only, it never writes to Health, and it never reads anything you didn’t allow.

Coutume keeps the daily total of each one with the habit it fills in, on your iPhone and in your iCloud. This data is never sent to us or to anyone else, and never used for advertising. You can change what Coutume reads at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Coutume.

What stays with you

Everything you keep in Coutume is stored on your device:

  • your habits, with their names, targets, icons and colors;
  • every day you logged, the slips you noted and the urges you resisted;
  • the daily totals read from Apple Health;
  • your levels, milestones and settings.

If iCloud is turned on for Coutume, this data is also synced with your private iCloud account, so you can find it on another iPhone. Apple keeps it under its own terms; we can’t access it. We have no server that keeps your data, and we can’t see it.

Widgets

Coutume’s widgets show the day’s rings on your Home Screen and Lock Screen. To do so, the app shares a short summary of your day with them, on your iPhone only. Like any widget, they can be seen by anyone who looks at your screen.

Sharing

You can share a milestone you’ve earned. This only happens when you ask, and it only goes where you choose to send it.

Usage statistics and crash reports

Coutume includes no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools. If you chose, in your iPhone’s settings, to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, which help us fix bugs. They don’t identify you and don’t include your habits or your Health data. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t ask for your name, your email address or an account.
  • We don’t show ads or track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don’t have public profiles, feeds or leaderboards.
  • We don’t sell or share your information, including your Health data.

Deleting your data

Since your data is on your iPhone and in your iCloud, you stay in control. You can delete a habit and its history in the app. Deleting Coutume erases its data from your device; to erase the iCloud copy too, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage. Earlier backups may still contain it until they’re replaced or deleted. Deleting Coutume doesn’t touch your data in Apple Health.

Children

Coutume collects no personal information, including from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Coutume handles information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.

Contact us

Wibeset is responsible for privacy in Coutume. For any privacy question or request, write to us at hello@wibeset.ca.
