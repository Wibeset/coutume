Your habits, right on your Home Screen

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Coutume’s widgets show the day’s rings and your streak of perfect days on your Home Screen, and fit on the Lock Screen too. A free habit tracker for iPhone, with no account and no ads.

The day at a glance

Each habit’s ring, as full as your day: glasses of water, steps filled in by Apple Health, the habits you quit, kept by default.

Your streak of perfect days

The widget also shows how many perfect days in a row you’ve kept, the days every habit was done.

Add the widget

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget, then choose Coutume. On the Lock Screen, touch and hold it, tap Customize, then add Coutume to the widgets under the clock.

Coutume is coming soon to the App Store, for iPhone: free, with no account, no ads and no subscription.
