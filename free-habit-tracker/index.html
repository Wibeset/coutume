A habit tracker that is free for real

Last updated: November 23, 2026

No trial that ends, no fourth habit behind a paywall, no ads between your rings. Coutume is a free habit tracker for iPhone, with no account and no subscription.

Everything, for free

Habits to build and to quit, Apple Health, forgiving streaks, levels, milestones, the calendar, widgets and iCloud sync: all of it, with no paid plan.

No account to create

Open the app and add your first habit. There is no sign-up, no email and no password, and nothing to delete later.

No ads, no tracking

Coutume has no ads, no analytics and no server of its own. Your habits stay on your iPhone and in your private iCloud, and we never receive them.

Why it’s free

Coutume is made by Wibeset, a small studio in Canada that makes apps it wants to use itself, like Reliure for reading. It is free because a habit tracker shouldn’t cost you a subscription.

Coutume is coming soon to the App Store, for iPhone: free, with no account, no ads and no subscription.
