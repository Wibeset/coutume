A “days without” counter for anything you quit

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Sugar, coffee, late-night scrolling, energy drinks: name what you want to quit, and Coutume counts your days without it. A free habit tracker for iPhone, with no account and no ads.

Any habit you want to quit

Create a habit to quit and give it a name and an icon. From then on, every day counts as kept unless you log a slip, and the count of days without grows on its own.

More than a number of days

Coutume also counts the money you save and the urges you resist, so a hard afternoon you got through counts for something.

A slip isn’t the end

A slip restarts the count, never your month or your level. Your milestones stay earned, and every day you kept still counts.

Several at once

Quit more than one thing, and build good habits beside them: they all share your day, your calendar and your levels.

Coutume is coming soon to the App Store, for iPhone: free, with no account, no ads and no subscription.
