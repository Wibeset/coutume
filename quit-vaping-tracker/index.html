A quit vaping tracker that counts what you gain

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Set “No vaping” as a habit to quit, and Coutume counts every day without it, the money you save and the urges you get through. Free, private, on iPhone.

Nothing to log on a good day

A habit to quit is kept by default. You don’t check anything when you didn’t vape; you only log the day you slip. Coutume does the counting.

Watch the days and the savings grow

Your days without vaping, the money you didn’t spend, and the urges you resisted this week, side by side on the habit’s page.

Every day kept still counts

A slip restarts the count of days without, but never takes away your level or your milestones. Eleven levels and eighteen milestones mark the way, one kept day after another.

See your month

The calendar shows each day of the month, brighter as more of your habits were kept: a quiet way to see how far you’ve come.

Coutume is a habit tracker, not a medical app or a quit-smoking program. For help to stop vaping, talk to a doctor, a pharmacist or your local quit line.

Coutume is coming soon to the App Store, for iPhone: free, with no account, no ads and no subscription.
