The best alternatives to Fabulous

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Fabulous coaches you through routines, but much of it sits behind a subscription. If you want to track your habits simply, for free and privately, here are the best alternatives, starting with Coutume.

1. Coutume: the best free alternative to Fabulous

Coutume doesn’t coach you: it keeps count. Tap a ring, let Apple Health fill in the rest, and watch your days add up to levels and milestones. Free, with no account and no subscription.

Why choose Coutume

  • Free, for good. No purchase, no subscription, no ads, and no habit limit behind a paywall.
  • Private by default. No server, no analytics: your habits stay on your iPhone and in your private iCloud. We never receive them.
  • Quick to log. Tap a ring and the day is logged; touch and hold it to enter a number or undo.
  • Bad habits get their own logic. A habit to quit is kept by default: you only log the day you slip. Coutume counts your days without, the money saved and the urges you resisted.
  • Apple Health does the logging. Steps, exercise minutes, mindful minutes and water fill in your habits on their own.

2. Productive

Best for planning routines by time of day. Habits are grouped into morning, afternoon and evening, with guided programs and challenges, on iPhone, Apple Watch and Android. Free with a habit limit; unlimited habits need a Premium subscription.

3. Finch

Best for gentle self-care with a virtual pet. You look after a little bird that grows as you finish your self-care goals, with mood check-ins, journaling and breathing exercises. Free, with an optional Finch Plus subscription.

4. Atoms

Best for readers of Atomic Habits. James Clear’s app helps you start small and tie each habit to a time and place, with short daily lessons. Atoms Pro costs US$4.99 a month or US$39.99 a year.

When Fabulous may suit you better

Fabulous is a coaching app as much as a tracker: guided journeys, morning routines, meditation, workouts and coaching sessions. You may prefer it if you want to be guided on what to do next.

Moving from Fabulous to Coutume

Coutume doesn’t import from Fabulous: add your habits again, it takes a few seconds each. Habits filled in by Apple Health catch up on their own, since Coutume reads the last seven days each time you open it.
