The best alternatives to Way of Life

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Way of Life is a fast green-or-red diary with good charts, but its free version limits your habits and you log everything yourself. Here are the best alternatives, starting with Coutume.

1. Coutume: the best free alternative to Way of Life

Coutume logs a habit in one tap too, and lets Apple Health fill in your steps, exercise, mindful minutes and water. Every habit is free, and levels and milestones reward each day kept.

Why choose Coutume

  • Free, for good. No purchase, no subscription, no ads, and no habit limit behind a paywall.
  • Apple Health does the logging. Steps, exercise minutes, mindful minutes and water fill in your habits on their own.
  • Bad habits get their own logic. A habit to quit is kept by default: you only log the day you slip. Coutume counts your days without, the money saved and the urges you resisted.
  • A streak that forgives. One missed day a week doesn’t break your streak, so one off day doesn’t erase weeks of effort.
  • Something to aim for. Eleven levels, from Good intention to Legend, and eighteen milestones, earned with every day kept.

2. Streaks

Best for a polished app across your Apple devices. A one-time purchase that runs on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Mac, with deep Apple Health support and up to 24 tasks, negative ones included. US$5.99, with no subscription.

3. HabitKit

Best for a private grid of your habits. No account, your data on your device, and a tile grid that fills in like a contribution graph. Free for 4 habits; Pro is monthly, yearly or lifetime.

4. Habitify

Best for tracking on iPhone, Android and the web. Your habits live in an online account and sync everywhere, with detailed statistics. Free for 3 habits; the Plus and Pro plans unlock the rest, and Apple Health is in Pro.

When Way of Life may suit you better

Way of Life has been around since 2010, runs on iPad and Android, and its charts and exports (CSV, Excel, JSON) go further. You may prefer it if you want to study trends over years, export your data or log from an Android phone.

Moving from Way of Life to Coutume

Coutume doesn’t import from Way of Life: add your habits again, it takes a few seconds each. Habits filled in by Apple Health catch up on their own, since Coutume reads the last seven days each time you open it.

Coutume is coming soon to the App Store, for iPhone: free, with no account, no ads and no subscription.
