How to drink 8 glasses of water a day

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Most people don’t dislike water: they just forget it. Drinking more is less about willpower than about putting a glass in the right places of your day.

Why it’s worth it

Water is the easiest habit to start with: no equipment, no time to find, and you can do it eight times a day. Because it’s simple, it becomes automatic sooner than most, and a habit that sticks early makes the next one easier.

How to start

  1. Pick a target you’ll reach. Eight glasses is a common goal, but if you drink three today, start with five. Your needs depend on your size, the weather and how much you move.
  2. Tie each glass to a moment. One when you wake up, one with each meal, one with your afternoon coffee, one when you get home. The moments do the remembering for you.
  3. Keep water in sight. A full bottle on your desk or in your bag gets drunk; a glass in the cupboard doesn’t.
  4. Make it something you like. Cold, sparkling, with lemon or mint: whatever makes you reach for it.
  5. Count as you go. Logging each glass shows you where you stand at 3 p.m., while there’s still time to catch up.

When it gets hard

  • You forget in the afternoon. Refill your bottle at lunch and set it where you work.
  • You don’t like plain water. Herbal tea and sparkling water count too.
  • You miss a day. It doesn’t matter much. What matters is the glass you drink tomorrow morning.
