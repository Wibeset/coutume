How to make your bed every morning

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Making the bed takes two minutes, yet most people skip it because, at 7 a.m., two minutes feel like a lot. The trick is to do it before you’ve had time to decide.

Why it’s worth it

A made bed is the first thing you finish in the day, and it makes the whole room look tidier with almost no effort. Coming home to it in the evening is a small pleasure that’s easy to underestimate.

How to start

  1. Do it as soon as you stand up. Before the bathroom, before your phone. Once you’ve left the room, coming back for it is much harder.
  2. Simplify the bedding. A duvet instead of sheets, blankets and a bedspread, and fewer decorative pillows. The fewer pieces, the faster it goes.
  3. Aim for good enough. Pull up the duvet, straighten the pillows, done. It doesn’t need to look like a hotel.
  4. Share the task. If you share the bed, the last one up makes it, or you do it together in thirty seconds.
  5. Use it as a starting signal. Once the bed is made, the day has begun. Some people find it gets them out of the snooze loop.

When it gets hard

  • You’re running late. Just pull the duvet up. Ten seconds still counts.
  • Someone is still sleeping. Make it as soon as they get up, or do it when you come back in the evening, before dinner.
  • You miss a day. Nothing happens. Tomorrow morning, make it again.
