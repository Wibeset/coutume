How to get outside in the morning light

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Most mornings go from bed to kitchen to car or bus without a minute spent outdoors. Not because it’s hard, but because nothing in the routine asks for it.

Why it’s worth it

Daylight early in the day helps set your body clock, which can make it easier to feel awake in the morning and sleepy at night. And a few quiet minutes outside before the day starts are pleasant on their own.

How to start

  1. Go out soon after you wake up. Within the first hour is a good aim. Even ten minutes on the balcony, the porch or the sidewalk counts.
  2. Pair it with something you already do. Drink your coffee outside, walk the dog, or get off the bus one stop early and walk the rest.
  3. Skip the sunglasses for a few minutes. Let your eyes take in the daylight, without looking straight at the sun.
  4. Go out even when it’s grey. Overcast mornings are still much brighter than indoor lighting. Dress for the weather and keep the habit.
  5. Have a winter plan. When it’s still dark at 7, go out at the first light you can, like a short walk at your morning break.

When it gets hard

  • You’re rushed in the morning. Combine it with your commute: walk part of the way, or wait for the bus outside instead of in the car.
  • It’s cold. Ten minutes is enough. Keep a warm coat and a tuque by the door so going out takes no planning.
  • You forget. Put your mug or your shoes by the door as a cue. The first thing you see in the morning decides a lot.
