How to stay off social media in the morning

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Checking your phone in bed takes no decision at all: the alarm is on it, so your thumb is already there. Ten minutes later, your morning belongs to other people’s posts.

Why it’s worth it

The first hour sets the tone of the day. Keeping it free of feeds gives you time to wake up, eat, think about what you want to do, and get to your own priorities before everyone else’s.

How to start

  1. Pick a clear time. “Not before 9 a.m.” is easy to check. “Not too much in the morning” isn’t.
  2. Charge your phone outside the bedroom. Use a cheap alarm clock. If the phone isn’t within reach, the first scroll doesn’t happen.
  3. Move the apps. Take them off your Home Screen, log out, or put them in a folder on the last page. A few extra taps are often enough to make you pause.
  4. Give your mornings a script. Water, shower, breakfast, a few pages of a book or a walk. When you know what comes next, the phone has less room.
  5. Keep what you actually need. Weather, calendar and messages from family can stay. The rule is about feeds, not about the phone.

When it gets hard

  • You use social media for work. Start work tasks after 9, or check only the work account from your computer.
  • You reach for it without thinking. Put the phone face down across the room, or in a drawer, until you’re dressed.
  • You slipped. Close the app as soon as you notice and log the slip. One morning doesn’t erase the others, and tomorrow starts the count again.
