40 habit ideas, to build and to quit

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Not sure which habits to track? Here are forty ideas, sorted by theme. Pick two or three, not ten: small habits you keep beat big ones you drop.

Health and movement

  • Drink 8 glasses of water. Count them one tap at a time, or let Apple Health fill them in.
  • Walk 10,000 steps, or 6,000 to start: a target you reach most days keeps the streak alive.
  • Move for 30 minutes. Apple Health counts your exercise minutes.
  • Stretch for 5 minutes after waking up.
  • Eat a fruit or a vegetable with every meal.
  • Take the stairs.
  • Cook dinner at home.
  • Take your vitamins or medication at the same time each day.

Sleep and evenings

  • Be in bed by 11 p.m.
  • No screens in bed.
  • Prepare tomorrow’s bag or clothes the night before.
  • Get outside in the morning light.
  • Write three lines about your day.

Mind and learning

  • Read 20 pages. Reliure, from the same makers, keeps track of your books.
  • Meditate for 10 minutes. Apple Health counts your mindful minutes.
  • Practise a language for 15 minutes.
  • Play an instrument.
  • Note one thing you’re grateful for.
  • Call or write to someone you care about.
  • Make your bed.
  • Tidy for 10 minutes.

Bad habits to quit

In Coutume, a habit to quit is kept by default: you only log the day you slip, and it counts your days without, the money saved and the urges you resisted.

  • No energy drinks.
  • No vaping, or no smoking.
  • No sugary drinks.
  • No snacking after dinner.
  • No coffee after noon.
  • No social media before 9 a.m.
  • No phone at the table.
  • No doomscrolling in bed.
  • No takeout on weeknights.
  • No impulse buys online.
  • No nail biting.
  • No hitting snooze.

Money

  • Write down every expense.
  • Bring your lunch.
  • Make your own coffee. As a habit to quit, “no café coffee” shows the money it saves.
  • Wait 24 hours before any purchase over $50.
  • Put aside $5 a day.
  • Check your accounts once a week.
  • Cancel one subscription you don’t use.

Make them stick

  • Start smaller than you think. Five minutes kept every day does more than an hour kept twice.
  • Tie it to something you already do: a glass of water with your coffee, a stretch after brushing your teeth.
  • Don’t chase a perfect streak. Missing one day barely matters; what matters is coming back the next one. That’s why Coutume forgives one missed day a week. Read how long it takes to form a habit.
