How to check your accounts

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Many people avoid looking at their bank account because they’re afraid of what they’ll see. The longer they wait, the bigger that fear gets.

Why it’s worth it

A quick look every day keeps surprises small: you notice the double charge, the forgotten renewal or the low balance while there’s still time to act. And the less you avoid it, the less stressful it becomes.

How to start

  1. Glance every day for two minutes. Open your banking app, look at your balance and yesterday’s transactions, and close it. That’s all.
  2. Pick a fixed moment. With your morning coffee, or on the bus home. The same moment every day turns it into a reflex.
  3. Check what you don’t recognize. A charge you can’t place gets a quick look right away, not at the end of the month.
  4. Take a longer look once a week. Sunday evening, for example: credit card, savings, upcoming bills. Fifteen minutes is enough.
  5. Keep it neutral. You’re looking at numbers, not grading yourself. Notice, adjust if needed, and move on.

When it gets hard

  • You’re afraid to look. Start with just the balance. Two minutes of knowing is easier than weeks of wondering.
  • You have too many accounts. Start with the main one, where your pay comes in. Add the others to your weekly look.
  • You see a problem. Write down what to do and when. Calling the bank tomorrow is fine; ignoring it is what hurts.
