How to meditate for 10 minutes a day

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Most people who give up on meditation think they’re doing it wrong because their mind keeps wandering. But noticing that it wandered and coming back is the whole exercise.

Why it’s worth it

Ten minutes of sitting still gives you a regular pause in a day that rarely has one. Many people find it makes them a little slower to react and a little quicker to notice how they feel, which helps long after the ten minutes are over.

How to start

  1. Start smaller than ten. If ten minutes feels long, sit for three and build up. A short session you do beats a long one you skip.
  2. Give it a fixed place and time. The same chair, right after you wake up or before you turn on your computer. The spot itself becomes the cue.
  3. Use a guide at first. A meditation app or the Mindfulness app on Apple Watch can walk you through the breathing so you don’t have to think about what to do.
  4. Expect your mind to wander. When you notice you’re planning dinner, gently come back to your breath. Each return is a repetition, like lifting a weight.
  5. Count every session. A distracted ten minutes still counts. The point is to show up, not to have a perfect sit.

When it gets hard

  • You’re too busy today. Sit for two minutes. Keeping the habit alive matters more than the length.
  • You get restless or sleepy. Sit up straighter, open your eyes a little, or try a walking meditation.
  • You miss a day. Don’t sit for twenty tomorrow to make up for it. Just sit for your ten.
