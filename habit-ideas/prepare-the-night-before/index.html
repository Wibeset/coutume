How to prepare the night before

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Mornings go wrong in the first ten minutes: the shirt that needs ironing, the keys nobody can find, the bag still half packed. Most of that could have been settled the evening before.

Why it’s worth it

Ten minutes in the evening saves more than ten minutes in the morning, because you do it calmly instead of in a rush. It also takes a few small decisions off your plate at the start of the day, and puts a clear end on the evening.

How to start

  1. Pick a fixed moment. Right after supper or right before brushing your teeth. Tying it to something you already do every night keeps you from forgetting it.
  2. Lay out your clothes. Check the weather, pick the outfit, and put it where you’ll see it. Include socks and anything that needs to be ironed.
  3. Pack the bag and leave it by the door. Laptop, charger, gym clothes, the papers to sign. Put your keys, wallet and badge on top or right beside it.
  4. Glance at tomorrow. One look at your calendar tells you if you need anything unusual: a lunch, a gift, an umbrella, an earlier start.
  5. Keep it short. Ten minutes is enough. The goal is a calmer morning, not a perfect plan for the day.

When it gets hard

  • You’re too tired at night. Do it right after supper, while you still have some energy, instead of waiting until bedtime.
  • You change your mind in the morning. That’s fine. Having a default ready is the point, even if you swap the sweater.
  • Your evenings are unpredictable. Keep a short list by the door of what always goes in the bag, so it takes two minutes even on a busy night.
