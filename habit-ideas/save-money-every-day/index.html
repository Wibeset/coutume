How to save money every day

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Saving often fails because it waits for what’s left at the end of the month, and there’s rarely anything left. Five dollars a day is small enough to come first.

Why it’s worth it

A small amount moved every day builds the habit of paying yourself first, and the total grows faster than you’d expect. It also gives you a cushion for the surprise bill, so it doesn’t have to go on a credit card.

How to start

  1. Open a separate account. A savings account you don’t see every time you pay for something. Money out of sight is money you don’t spend by accident.
  2. Move the $5 at the same time every day. With your morning coffee, or right after lunch. It takes less than a minute in your banking app.
  3. Give it a name. “Emergency fund”, “Summer trip”, “New bike”. Many banks let you rename an account, and a name makes it harder to dip into.
  4. Adjust the amount to your situation. If $5 is too much right now, start with $2. If it’s easy, raise it. The daily gesture matters more than the amount.
  5. Leave it alone. Decide in advance what counts as a reason to use it, like a real emergency or the goal you named.

When it gets hard

  • Money is tight this week. Move $1. Keeping the gesture keeps the habit, and you can go back to $5 next week.
  • You forget. Tie it to a daily moment and put the Coutume widget on your Home Screen, next to your banking app.
  • You need to take some out. That’s what it’s there for. Use it without guilt and start again the next day.

Track it in Coutume

In Coutume, add “$5 set aside” as a habit to build and check it off once the transfer is done. Your level rises as the days add up, from Good intention to Legend.
