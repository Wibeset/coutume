How to order less takeout on weeknights

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Takeout rarely wins because you crave it. It wins at 6:30 p.m., when you’re tired, the fridge looks empty, and the app is one tap away.

Why it’s worth it

Weeknight takeout adds up quietly, one delivery fee at a time. Keeping it for the weekend turns it back into a treat, and the money you don’t spend becomes something you can actually see.

How to start

  1. Have three fallback meals. Pasta with a jar of sauce, eggs and toast, a quick stir-fry. Meals you can make tired, in twenty minutes, without a recipe.
  2. Stock the freezer. Frozen vegetables, dumplings, a batch of soup you cooked on Sunday. On a hard night, the freezer is your takeout.
  3. Decide dinner in the morning. Take something out to thaw before work. A plan made at 8 a.m. beats a decision made hungry at 6 p.m.
  4. Move the delivery apps. Delete them from your phone, or at least log out. Ordering from a browser is slower, and slower is the point.
  5. Keep a weekend night for it. You’re not giving takeout up. Choose the night you’ll enjoy it, and the weeknights get easier.

When it gets hard

  • You get home late and exhausted. That’s what the freezer meal is for. Heat it before you open any app.
  • Someone else wants to order. Suggest moving takeout to Friday, or cook your fallback meal for both of you.
  • You slipped. Enjoy the meal, log the slip, and restock one fallback for next time. One order doesn’t erase the money you’ve already saved.
