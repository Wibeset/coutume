The best alternatives to Productive

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Productive plans your habits by time of day, but unlimited habits need its Premium subscription. If you want a simpler tracker that costs nothing, here are the best alternatives, starting with Coutume.

1. Coutume: the best free alternative to Productive

Coutume is a free habit tracker for iPhone: every habit, levels, milestones, widgets and Apple Health, with no subscription, no account and no ads.

Why choose Coutume

  • Free, for good. No purchase, no subscription, no ads, and no habit limit behind a paywall.
  • No account, no sign-up. Open the app and add your first habit. No password to remember, no profile to manage.
  • Apple Health does the logging. Steps, exercise minutes, mindful minutes and water fill in your habits on their own.
  • Bad habits get their own logic. A habit to quit is kept by default: you only log the day you slip. Coutume counts your days without, the money saved and the urges you resisted.
  • A streak that forgives. One missed day a week doesn’t break your streak, so one off day doesn’t erase weeks of effort.

2. Streaks

Best for a polished app across your Apple devices. A one-time purchase that runs on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Mac, with deep Apple Health support and up to 24 tasks, negative ones included. US$5.99, with no subscription.

3. Habitify

Best for tracking on iPhone, Android and the web. Your habits live in an online account and sync everywhere, with detailed statistics. Free for 3 habits; the Plus and Pro plans unlock the rest, and Apple Health is in Pro.

4. Atoms

Best for readers of Atomic Habits. James Clear’s app helps you start small and tie each habit to a time and place, with short daily lessons. Atoms Pro costs US$4.99 a month or US$39.99 a year.

When Productive may suit you better

Productive groups habits into morning, afternoon and evening, offers guided programs and challenges, and runs on Apple Watch and Android. You may prefer it if you plan your day around routines or want to log from your wrist.

Moving from Productive to Coutume

Coutume doesn’t import from Productive: add your habits again, it takes a few seconds each. Habits filled in by Apple Health catch up on their own, since Coutume reads the last seven days each time you open it.
