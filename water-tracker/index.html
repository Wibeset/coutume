A water tracker that takes one tap per glass

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Set a daily target, like 8 glasses of water, and tap the ring each time you drink. Or let Apple Health fill it in. Coutume is a free habit tracker for iPhone, with no account and no ads.

One tap, one glass

A habit you count adds one with each tap, and its ring fills toward your target. Touch and hold the ring to enter a number or undo the last glass.

Or let Apple Health count

If you already log water in Health, or with an app or a bottle that writes to it, link your water habit to Health and it fills itself in.

A streak that forgives

Each day you reach your target extends your streak, and one missed day a week is forgiven. The calendar shows your month, brighter as more habits were kept.

With your other habits

Water sits beside your steps, your reading or your bedtime, in the same day and on the same Home Screen widget.

Coutume is coming soon to the App Store, for iPhone: free, with no account, no ads and no subscription.
