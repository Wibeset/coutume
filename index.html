Questions, answered

Price

Is Coutume free?

Yes. Coutume is free, with no ads, no subscription and no account.

Account

Do I need an account?

No. Coutume works without an account or sign-up. Your habits are stored on your iPhone and synced with your private iCloud, which we can’t access.

Logging

How do I log a habit?

Tap its ring. A habit you count, like 8 glasses of water, adds one with each tap; touch and hold the ring to enter a number or undo. Steps, exercise minutes, mindful minutes and water can fill themselves in from Apple Health.

Quitting

How do habits to quit work?

Every day counts as kept unless you log a slip. Coutume counts your days without, the money you’ve saved and the urges you resisted. A slip restarts the count, never your month or your level.

Streaks

What if I miss a day?

Your streak forgives one missed day per week. Miss more, and the streak starts over, but your level and your milestones stay: they count every day you’ve kept.

Apple Health

What does Coutume read from Health?

Only what you allow: steps, exercise minutes, mindful minutes and water. Coutume never writes to Health, and your totals stay on your iPhone and in your iCloud.

Backups

What if I change iPhones?

Your habits sync with your iCloud and come back on your new iPhone, with all their history, levels and milestones.

Android

Is Coutume available on Android?

No. Coutume is available for iPhone, in English and French.
