A Habitify alternative, without an account or subscription

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Coutume is a free habit tracker for iPhone. No account to create, no plan to choose and no subscription: your habits stay on your iPhone and in your private iCloud.

Coutume and Habitify at a glance

PriceFree, no adsFree plan for up to 3 habits; Plus and Pro plans, monthly, yearly or lifetime

Based on publicly available information as of November 2026: Habitify moved to Free, Plus and Pro plans in April 2026, and prices vary by country. Habitify is a trademark of its owner; Coutume is not affiliated with it.

Why choose Coutume

  • Every habit, free. No plan to upgrade to add a fourth habit, and no ads.
  • No account, no sign-up. Open the app and add your first habit. There is no password to remember and no profile to manage.
  • Private by default. No server, no analytics: we never receive your habits or your Health data.
  • Built to quit, too. A habit to quit is kept by default, and Coutume counts your days without, the money saved and the urges you resisted.
  • A streak that forgives. One missed day a week is forgiven, and your levels and milestones count every day you kept.

When Habitify may suit you better

Habitify works on Android, Mac and the web, and keeps your habits in an online account. You may prefer it if you switch between an iPhone and an Android phone or a computer, or if you want the detailed reports of its paid plans.

Moving from Habitify

Coutume doesn’t import from Habitify: add your habits again, it takes a few seconds each. Habits filled in by Apple Health catch up on their own, since Coutume reads the last seven days each time you open it.

Coutume is coming soon to the App Store, for iPhone: free, with no account, no ads and no subscription.
