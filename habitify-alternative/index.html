The best alternatives to Habitify

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Habitify’s free plan stops at three habits, and Apple Health is part of its Pro plan. If you want every habit for free, without an account, here are the best alternatives, starting with Coutume.

1. Coutume: the best free alternative to Habitify

Coutume is a free habit tracker for iPhone. No account to create, no plan to choose and no subscription: your habits stay on your iPhone and in your private iCloud.

Why choose Coutume

  • Free, for good. No purchase, no subscription, no ads, and no habit limit behind a paywall.
  • No account, no sign-up. Open the app and add your first habit. No password to remember, no profile to manage.
  • Private by default. No server, no analytics: your habits stay on your iPhone and in your private iCloud. We never receive them.
  • Bad habits get their own logic. A habit to quit is kept by default: you only log the day you slip. Coutume counts your days without, the money saved and the urges you resisted.
  • A streak that forgives. One missed day a week doesn’t break your streak, so one off day doesn’t erase weeks of effort.

2. Streaks

Best for a polished app across your Apple devices. A one-time purchase that runs on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Mac, with deep Apple Health support and up to 24 tasks, negative ones included. US$5.99, with no subscription.

3. Way of Life

Best for a quick yes-or-no diary with strong charts. Each day you mark a habit green or red, and the charts show the trend over months. Around since 2010, on iPhone, iPad and Android. Free with a habit limit; Premium unlocks the rest.

4. Strides

Best for mixing habits with goals and targets. Besides habits, it tracks targets by a date, averages and projects, with over 150 templates, Apple Health, a Mac app and a web app. Free for 3 trackers; Strides Plus is a subscription or a lifetime purchase.

When Habitify may suit you better

Habitify works on Android, Mac and the web, and keeps your habits in an online account. You may prefer it if you switch between an iPhone and an Android phone or a computer, or if you want the detailed reports of its paid plans.

Moving from Habitify to Coutume

Coutume doesn’t import from Habitify: add your habits again, it takes a few seconds each. Habits filled in by Apple Health catch up on their own, since Coutume reads the last seven days each time you open it.

Coutume is coming soon to the App Store, for iPhone: free, with no account, no ads and no subscription.
